Overview

The POC pipeline includes the following components:

  • OTCM (OpenText Content Management System) – source of logs, metrics, and trace-like data

  • Fluent Bit – lightweight log shipper to collect OTCM logs and system events

  • OpenTelemetry Collector – central pipeline to process, transform, and export logs and metrics

  • Prometheus & Loki – backends for storing metrics and logs

  • Grafana – optional visualization layer for dashboards

Telemetry flows from OTCM logs and system metrics into Fluent Bit, then into various OpenTelemetry collectors. The collectors process, transform, and forward the data to Prometheus and Loki. Grafana reads from Prometheus and Loki to visualize logs, metrics, and derived insights.
