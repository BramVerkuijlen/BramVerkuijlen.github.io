Monitoring Expansion : Recommendations

Created by Bram Verkuijlen, last modified on dec 29, 2025

This section provides recommendations based on the Monitoring Expansion Proof of Concept (POC) and the research performed during the internship. It is intended for engineers, architects, and decision-makers at AmeXio who are evaluating monitoring strategies for OpenText Content Management (OTCM) and broader observability adoption.

Purpose

This section provides guidance on how to use the Monitoring Expansion POC effectively and how to use its findings for broader observability strategies. Topics include:

  • Best practices for collecting performance data from OpenText Content Server (OTCM)

  • Strategies for integrating the POC with AMI (AmeXio Monitoring Insights)

  • Recommendations for deploying the POC on other systems or operating systems

  • Lessons learned for using, extending, and maintaining the POC

Scope

The information provided here are based on hands-on research and experimentation during the internship project. They are meant for:

  • Understanding capabilities and limitations of the current POC

  • Informing architectural decisions and integration strategies

  • Guiding future extensions of the POC or AMI

This section does not provide exhaustive implementation instructions, and assumes familiarity with the POC’s architecture and components.
